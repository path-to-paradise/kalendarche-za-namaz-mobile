## English

**App name:** Prayer Times (Времето за намаз)

**Short description** (80 max):
```
Accurate prayer times for 48 cities across Bulgaria
```

**Full description:**
```
Prayer Times shows accurate daily prayer times — Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib,
and Isha — plus Sunrise, Duha, and Tahajjud, for 48 cities across Bulgaria.

KEY FEATURES

• Accurate prayer times for 48 cities — from Sofia and Plovdiv to smaller
  towns like Kaolinovo and Nikopol
• Swipe up/down between days, with a quick "back to today" shortcut
• Live highlighting of the current prayer
• Ramadan day counter and Eid al-Fitr banners
• Three prayer-naming styles — default, Arabic, or descriptive — to match
  your mosque or community's tradition
• Light, dark, or system theme, with 10 color options
• Full Bulgarian and English language support
• Works fully offline — prayer-time data is bundled into the app, no
  internet connection required

The app requires no account, shows no ads, and collects no personal data.
See the full privacy policy linked from the app listing.

Feedback and source code:
https://github.com/path-to-paradise/kalendarche-za-namaz-mobile
```
